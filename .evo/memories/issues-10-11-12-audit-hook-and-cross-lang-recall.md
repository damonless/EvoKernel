## Symptom
1) Workspaces with 600+ token coreDigest lost Section 5 micro-tools and failed evo audit (Issue #14); 2) Antigravity hook missed source='MODEL' GENERIC outputs, read-only/1..3-edit diagnostic investigations, subagent wakeups, rhetorical/empirical corrections, and resurrected cleared triggers after >128KB transcript tail truncation (Issues #14-#15); 3) Agents stopped at L0 summaries without calling evo_inspect(tier: 'L2') or skipped crystallization in read-only troubleshooting sessions (Issue #15).

## Root Cause
1) Issues #10-#14: agy-config-auditor compared core-invariants.md in Split-Brain, failed on Class.method or file:symbol targets, and conflated maxCoreRuleTokens (1500) with static seed tokens, while syncManagedRuleBlocks/runInit/runAudit omitted rules/AGENTS.md, handlePropose skipped syncManagedRuleBlocks on skill proposals, renderManagedBlock read stale in-memory L0_META_RULE_SEED in long-lived MCP servers, and telemetry omitted reverse English keys log/version; 2) antigravity_hook.mjs filtered out source='MODEL' GENERIC tool outputs, ignored zero-edit read-only investigations (or left a 1..3 editCount dead zone) and native subagent wakeups, returned early before persisting cleared watermarks on evo_propose (resurrecting cleared corrections/audits after >128KB tail truncation), and missed grep_search, relative meta paths, and virtualized codegraph calls; 3) L0_META_RULE_SEED omitted git commit/deliverable recall triggers, conditioned evo_propose only on code fixes, handleSearch emitted a passive L0 tip instead of requiring L2 inspection on matched rules/skills/verified memories, and mcp_config.json, .claude-plugin/plugin.json, and gemini-extension.json lacked zero-build auto-compilation when dist/ was unbuilt in a fresh clone.

## Solution
1) Decouple Section 4 maxCoreRuleTokens (1500) from MAX_TOOLS_CATALOG_TOKENS (350), align agy-config-auditor managedBlockBudgetLimit with audit.ts, include rules/AGENTS.md across syncManagedRuleBlocks/runInit/runAudit/agy-config-auditor, trigger syncManagedRuleBlocks on skill proposals in handlePropose, read latest on-disk L0_META_RULE_SEED in renderManagedBlock, and add reverse English synonym keys log/version in BILINGUAL_TECH_SYNONYMS; 2) Remove nextSource !== 'MODEL', index steps[i+1+tcIdx], track codeReadCountSinceLastPropose >= 10 with editCountSinceLastPropose < 4 (including grep_search and virtualized codegraph evo_run while ignoring relative .evo/.agents/.gemini paths), detect SYSTEM_MESSAGE subagent wakeups, persist cleared*Step watermarks before early return when evo_propose is seen, and expand CORRECTION_REGEX for rhetorical counter-questions and empirical contradictions; 3) Mandate read-only diagnostic crystallization and L2 guardrail inspection in L0_META_RULE_SEED, emit Action Required L2 directives in handleSearch, and wire mcp_config.json, .claude-plugin/plugin.json, and gemini-extension.json to auto-build dist/ via scripts/boot-mcp.mjs when dist/cli/index.js is missing.
