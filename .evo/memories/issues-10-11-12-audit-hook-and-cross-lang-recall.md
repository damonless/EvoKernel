## Symptom
1) agy-config-auditor failed on core-invariants.md divergence, string-literal symbols, Class.method/file:symbol targets, or missing fileHint symbols, while core:true rules lost core flag on compact/upgrade (Issues #10 & #13). 2) Multi-step Antigravity turns either lost evolution reminders after step 1 or spammed every step by bypassing COOLDOWN_STEPS=4, and missed run_command failures in steps[i+1] (Issues #11 & #13). 3) Chinese hardware queries missed English tools while lowercase tokens ('L0', 'u8', 'k8s', hex hashes) falsely triggered hardware expansions, and host files exceeded 1,500 tokens on >6 tools or near-limit core rules (Issues #12 & #13).

## Root Cause
1) Issue #10 & #13: agy-config-auditor compared core-invariants.md in Split-Brain, used naive comment stripping, failed on Class.method or file:symbol targets, masked missing fileHint symbols via workspace fallback, and hardcoded 1,500-token budgets; evaluateAndCommit/handleCompact lost transfer_scenarios or core:true on upgrade/compact; tokenizeClauseText treated CJK sentences as single tokens. 2) Issue #11 & #13: antigravity_hook.mjs consumed state on first PreInvocation or bypassed COOLDOWN_STEPS=4 on every subsequent step, missed run_command outputs in steps[i+1], and failed to unquote double-JSON transcript args. 3) Issue #12 & #13: extractQueryTokens lacked ZH<->EN synonyms or false-positived on lowercase L0/L1/L2/u8/k8s/hex hashes due to case-insensitive HW_DESIGNATOR_REGEX; syncManagedRuleBlocks/evaluateAndCommit had split-brain host vs core token budgets.

## Solution
1) Exclude core-invariants.md in Audit 4, use state-machine comment+string stripping and dotted/colon symbol matching without cross-file fallback in agy-config-auditor, inherit transfer_scenarios and core:true on rule upgrade/compact, and unify host/core token budget checks across gatekeeper.ts, init.ts, audit.ts, and agy-config-auditor. 2) Defer clearing correction, dirty-edit, and error-recovery state until Stop or evo_propose while enforcing COOLDOWN_STEPS=4 after initial edge triggers and reading steps[i+1] command outputs in antigravity_hook.mjs. 3) Make HW_DESIGNATOR_REGEX case-sensitive and unit regexes boundary-guarded in extractQueryTokens, persist/index tool tags and schema properties, and inject budget-bounded Section 5 tool catalog in syncManagedRuleBlocks.
