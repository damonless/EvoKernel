## Symptom
1) agy-config-auditor exited 1 on core-invariants.md divergence or missed deleted symbols present in string literals, upgraded scoped rules lost transfer_scenarios, evo_compact bypassed Gate 1.5 and Clause Diff, and Chinese clause edits triggered 100% false-positive orthogonal clause warnings (Issue #10). 2) Multi-step Antigravity turns lost evolution reminders after step 1 or >128KB transcript tails, double-JSON args broke path/tool matching, and .xls/.xlsx deliverable feedback failed to trigger crystallization (Issue #11). 3) Chinese hardware queries ('CN3008 12V 调试电源 电源测试口 电压') returned 0 hits for English hardware tools ('3ku-hw-query'), workspace tools were invisible in AGENTS.md/GEMINI.md or exceeded 1,500 tokens in .cursor/rules/evo-kernel.mdc when >6 tools existed, and duplicate rule IDs crashed capability_fts_fallback with UNIQUE constraint failed (Issue #12).

## Root Cause
1) Issue #10: agy-config-auditor compared core-invariants.md in Split-Brain and used naive regex comment stripping that matched symbols in strings; ensureGlobalFallbackSeeded skipped updating changed seed files; evaluateAndCommit lost transfer_scenarios on upgrade and bypassed Gate 1.5/Clause Diff on evo_compact; runRefactor --apply ignored globalGateViolations; tokenizeClauseText treated CJK sentences as single tokens. 2) Issue #11: antigravity_hook.mjs consumed lastCorrectionStep/lastDirtyRemindedStep/lastErrorRecoveryStep on the first PreInvocation before Stop/verification, ignored read-only investigation steps after user correction, lost pending corrections across >128KB transcript tails, failed to unquote double-JSON-serialized transcript args, and root hooks.json lacked CWD-resilient path fallback. 3) Issue #12: extractQueryTokens lacked ZH<->EN technical synonyms and PCB designator/unit expansion (or truncated expansions on >30-bigram Chinese queries); tool manifests lacked tags and ID/schema FTS indexing; syncManagedRuleBlocks omitted workspace tool L0 catalog or overflowed 1,500-token budget on >6 tools; capability_fts_fallback used plain INSERT without deduplicating rule IDs.

## Solution
1) Exclude core-invariants.md in Audit 4 and copyDirNonOverwriting, use state-machine comment+string stripping and word-boundary symbol matching in agy-config-auditor, sync seed files in ensureGlobalFallbackSeeded, remediate globalGateViolations in runRefactor --apply, inherit transfer_scenarios on rule upgrade/compact, enforce Gate 1.5 and preserve supersededRules clause diff in evo_compact, and use CJK 2-grams with stripped Case Anchors in tokenizeClauseText. 2) Defer clearing correction, dirty-edit, and error-recovery state until Stop or evo_propose in antigravity_hook.mjs, count read/search investigation steps after user correction, persist lastCorrectionStep across >128KB transcript tails, unquote double-JSON transcript strings, and deploy CWD-resilient hooks.json in root and .agents/hooks.json. 3) Partition technical CJK bigrams and reserve expansion budget in extractQueryTokens, persist and index tool tags/ID/schema properties, inject budget-bounded Section 5 tool catalog in syncManagedRuleBlocks + handleSearch footer, and deduplicate listRules/INSERT OR REPLACE in capability_fts_fallback.
