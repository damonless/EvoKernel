When finishing any task or milestone, keep the delivery summary concise, direct, and low-friction for human review:
1. State what changed in plain language using 3-5 short bullet points (problem fixed, files touched, and verification result).
2. Avoid verbose narrative, marketing prose, or multi-table walls of text unless explicitly requested.
3. Highlight only actionable items or decisions that require user attention.
